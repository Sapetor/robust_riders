# Scoring System

The Physical Stage scoring combines **ride value** and **rating** to calculate earnings.

---

## 💰 Formula

```
Ride Value × Rating = Total Money Earned
```

Where:
- **Ride Value** = Dollar amount based on ride complexity
- **Rating** = Stars earned (1-5 scale)

---

## ⭐ Rating System

All rides start with **5 stars**. Stars are deducted for infractions.

### Infractions

| Infraction Type | Description | Impact |
|-----------------|-------------|--------|
| **Lane Violations** | Crossing over lane lines | -⭐ |
| **Stop Violations** | Not fully stopping at traffic controls | -⭐ |
| **Timing Violations** | Slow reaction to traffic controls | -⭐ |
| **LED Errors** | Wrong LED color for action | -⭐ |
| **Pickup/Drop-off Errors** | Not stopping properly | -⭐ |
| **Obstacle Collision** | Hitting obstacles | -⭐ |

> [!WARNING]
> This list may be expanded during the competition. Judges will notify teams of any additions.

---

## 💵 Ride Complexity

Rides have different dollar values based on complexity:

| Complexity | Example | Typical Value |
|------------|---------|---------------|
| **Simple** | Direct A→B route | Lower |
| **Medium** | Route with turns | Medium |
| **Complex** | Multiple stops, complex navigation | Higher |

> [!NOTE]
> Actual ride values are announced on competition day.

---

## 🔄 Multiple Attempts

- You can attempt the same ride **multiple times**
- Only the **best rating** for each ride counts
- Higher rating = more money for that ride

### Strategy Considerations

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **Perfect one ride** | Maximum points for that ride | Time spent on single ride |
| **Complete many rides** | More total ride values | Risk of lower ratings |
| **Balance** | Mix of rides + quality | Requires good time management |

---

## 🏆 Winning Strategy

To maximize your score:

1. **Prioritize accuracy** - Fewer infractions = higher ratings
2. **Know the routes** - Practice in QLabs simulation
3. **Speed matters** - More rides = more potential earnings
4. **LED discipline** - Correct colors every time
5. **Clean stops** - Full stops at all required points

---

## 📋 Judge Tracking

Judges track:
- Completed rides
- Rating for each ride
- Infractions observed
- Final score calculation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All judge decisions are **final** unless overruled by another judge.
