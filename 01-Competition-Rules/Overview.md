# Competition Overview

The **2026 ACC Self-Driving Car Student Competition** is organized by [Quanser](https://www.quanser.com/) and takes place at the **American Control Conference** in **New Orleans, Louisiana, USA** from **May 26-28, 2026**.

---

## 🎯 Main Objective

Create a **self-driving algorithm** capable of navigating through **Quanser City** as an **autonomous taxi service**. Teams must maximize profits by completing taxi rides efficiently within a time limit.

---

## 📊 Competition Structure

The competition consists of **three stages**:

### Stage 1: Virtual Competition (Qualifying)
> **Deadline: February 27, 2026**

- All registered teams participate automatically
- Use **QLabs** (Quanser Interactive Labs) digital twin environment
- Create a **3-minute video** demonstrating your self-driving algorithm
- Submit: **GitHub link** + **YouTube video**

### Stage 2: Physical QCar 2 Validation
> **March - May 2026**

- Finalists selected from Stage 1 receive access to physical QCar 2
- Validate your algorithm on physical hardware
- Details released closer to the event

### Stage 3: In-Person Competition
> **May 26-28, 2026 | New Orleans, Louisiana**

- Live competition at the ACC conference venue
- Compete on physical Quanser roadmaps
- Maximize taxi ride profits in real-time

---

## 📋 Team Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Minimum team size** | 1 student + 1 faculty advisor |
| **Maximum team size** | No limit (QLabs access for first 10 members) |
| **University affiliation** | Required |

---

## 🏆 Awards

The winning team receives:

1. **QCar 2** - Physical 1/10 scale autonomous vehicle for the faculty's lab
2. **QLabs Virtual QCar** - 1-year subscription
3. **$2,000 Cash Prize** - Divided among team members
4. **Golden QCar Trophy** - Recognition of excellence

---

## 📈 Ranking Criteria

Teams will be ranked based on:

1. **Readiness** of Self-Driving algorithm (core principles)
2. **Accuracy** of driving (staying within lanes)
3. **Timely reaction** to road signage and traffic controls
4. **Clear communication** of Self-Driving concepts in video

> [!TIP]
> The communication criteria is critical - judges evaluate how well your team understands and explains self-driving principles.
